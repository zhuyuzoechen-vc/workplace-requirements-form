Paper & workflow processes

NXP Austin_Next is designed around a paper-independent, digital-first way of working. Please review the standard approach and flag any genuine exceptions your team cannot accommodate.

What the NXP Austin standard already includes
  • Ongoing shift to digital workflows — physical signing, filing, and paper distribution is being replaced by digital equivalents wherever feasible
  • Follow-me / pull printing: Documents only print when you authenticate at a printer of your choice. User authentication is required to retrieve documents or scan to email.
  • Continuing reduction in team paper storage — moving toward paper-independent working aligned with ABW principles
  • Centralized mailroom: All mail and packages delivered to a central point; teams collect or request digital routing
  • NXP has an active internal initiative to digitize materials — teams are expected to align with this direction

Storage needs

Austin_Next is designed around Activity Based Working (ABW), which requires a shift away from individual assigned storage. Here's what's already built into the plan.

What the NXP Austin standard already includes
  • Personal lockers available for employees — capacity up to the size of a wireless keyboard; available to all staff and visitors for day use
  • Locking lateral filing cabinets allocated to teams and individuals based on demonstrated need — not a universal per-person allocation
  • Non-workflow storage room available for items not needed day-to-day (statutory documents, marketing collateral, stationery, seasonal items)
  • Long-term archiving held off-site where items don't need regular on-site access

Topic 4 of 8

Access, confidentiality & security

NXP Austin uses a structured security zone model. Many confidentiality needs are already addressed by the design — read carefully before flagging an exception.

What the NXP Austin standard already includes

NXP Austin uses a 4-level security zone model:

Level 1 – Blue: Public areas, no access controls
Level 2 – Green: Standard badge access for general office areas
Level 3 – Yellow: Restricted badge access for confidential functions (HR, Finance, Legal) or secured labs
Level 4 – Red: Badge + PIN, continuous monitoring for high-security areas
  • Confidential Neighborhoods: frosted/semi-transparent treatment, acoustic soundproofing, screen protectors at open workstations, badge-secured entry — located in low-traffic peripheral areas
  • Every employee has access to acoustic privacy (phone booths, enclosed rooms) for sensitive conversations
  • Every employee has access to visual privacy options (privacy screen filters, enclosed booths)
  • Training provided on NXP Global Security Guidelines and zone-specific requirements
Does the standard NXP security and confidentiality provision cover your team's needs?
Consider: specific data classification requirements (e.g. ITAR, export control), regulatory constraints on adjacency or co-location, physical security needs beyond standard zone badge access, or visitor restriction requirements beyond the norm.

Meeting spaces

Austin_Next includes a full range of meeting environments. Please flag only requirements that go beyond this standard provision.

What NXP Austin_Next already includes
  • Formal Meeting Spaces (6–20 people): Acoustically private, reservable, built-in AV with Microsoft Teams (Logitech/Jabra certified), 1 power outlet per chair — accessible to all neighborhoods from the building core
  • Small Meeting Spaces (2–4 people): Bookable and non-bookable enclosed rooms with varying AV setup to support small huddles or meetings
  • Focus Rooms (1–2 people): Non-reservable enclosed rooms, max 2-hour use, acoustic privacy, sit-stand desks, 24" monitor + webcam
  • Alternative Meeting Spaces (6+ people): Reservable medium/large rooms with flexible or informal furniture supporting hybrid collaboration with built-in or mobile AV
  • Collaboration Hubs & Social Hearts: Non-reservable open informal settings for small-group work, solo focus, and social interaction
  • Note: Training rooms and auditorium space will be provided at the Champion site.

Topic 7 of 8

Ergonomics & accessibility

Austin_Next is designed to a higher ergonomics standard than most existing NXP workplaces. Individual health-related accommodations should go through NXP's occupational health team, not this form.

What NXP Austin_Next already includes
  • All focus and process workstations will be sit-to-stand height-adjustable.
  • Task chairs with full adjustability: adjustable arms, lumbar support, rolling casters, adjustable seat height — rated for 8-hour use
  • Designed to meet or exceed wheelchair accessibility standards throughout the space
  • A choice of environments means employees can self-select brighter/dimmer areas, quieter/more energetic zones throughout the day
  • Individuals with an endorsed requirement through NXP's occupational health team will be managed through that existing process — do not include personal health details in this form
Does the standard ergonomic design cover your team's group-level needs?
Flag here only if your team has a functional (not personal health) requirement — e.g. a specialist workstation configuration required by the role itself, not by an individual's medical need.
